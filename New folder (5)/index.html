About Us

St Jois Daycare is a trusted early childhood center based in Jovena, Singida. Our goal is to give children a safe, nurturing space to learn, grow, and flourish.

  • Qualified and caring caregivers

  • Nutritious meals and snacks

  • Outdoor play areas
